Key Proof Points
🤝
Not blind automation
Every remediation decision is backed by CMDB criticality, error tolerance policy, and a 6-check safety gate. The platform explains its reasoning at every step.
🔬
Test before you touch
Drift snapshots capture the pre-fix state. Every remediation is tested on a clone first. The live system is only touched after the snapshot verifies clean.
📊
Cross-client intelligence
Peer insights surface how similar incidents resolved across your entire client portfolio — anonymised. Your team learns from every incident across every tenant.
🏛️
Audit-ready by default
Every action produces evidence: approval events, Ansible logs, before/after state diffs, MTTR, SLA compliance. Nothing happens off the record.
⚡
Minutes, not hours
From first alert to verified closure in under 15 minutes for redundant systems. Human-approved paths add review time — the platform never adds unnecessary delay.
🔗
Integrates with your stack
ServiceNow CMDB, Prometheus, Grafana, Ansible, and your existing alert pipelines. No rip-and-replace — the platform wraps around what you already have.
